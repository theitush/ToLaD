# Felidae: the data's dated tree vs the old hand tree (ToLaD#145)

The fan's cat tree used to be typed into web/src/fan/splits.ts (TimeTree 5 pairs outside Panthera, Figueiro 2017 inside).
It now comes from the pipeline's dated tree (pipeline/FORMAT.md, "Dated tree"): Upham et al. 2019's node-dated MCC
tree, fossils grafted by rule, and the hand values left in pipeline/overrides.toml ([[dated_clade]] Panthera and the
domestic cat, [[dated_fossil]] four Panthera fossils). Generated by a one-off script (first build, before main's datafix
merge; the living cats and the Upham dates are unchanged by it).

**Why the dates differ.** Outside Panthera every hand date was TimeTree 5's pairwise synthesis age; the data's are
Upham's node heights. Upham dates the cat family much younger: crown Felidae 7.2 Ma (95% HPD 5.2-9.1) against TimeTree's
13.8 (11.95-15.66) and Johnson et al. 2006's 10.8. The ranges do not overlap at the deep splits (crown, Pantherinae,
Felinae, bay cat, lynx, leopard cat lineages). Upham's tree is a 31-gene supermatrix dated with node calibrations across
all mammals; it is not tuned for cats. If the deep cat dates matter more than a uniform source, the fix is one
[[dated_clade]] entry per clade (Li et al. 2016 or Johnson 2006), not code.

**Topology.** Upham pairs differently in several places: Pardofelis with the bay cat (not the golden cats), Pallas's cat
with Felis, the ocelot lineage resolved as (tigrinus, guigna), (geoffroyi, colocolo), (jacobita, (pardalis, wiedii)),
Lynx as (lynx, (pardinus, canadensis)). Panthera is Figueiro's (override). The southern tiger cat (not in Upham) hangs
from the Leopardus stem at its interpolated date (source: interpolated, low confidence).

**Species.** The data adds every Felidae fossil it dates (sabre-tooths, Pseudaelurus, Felis/Lynx/Acinonyx fossils);
the root moves to ~19 Ma (Pseudaelurus/Styriofelis). Fossils the data cannot place inside a crown join that crown's
split as extra daughters ("loose"; the card says so).
